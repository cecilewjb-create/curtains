Precision Tailoring

The Measurement & Hanging Guide

Follow our 3-step master guidelines. We provide complimentary "Sure-Fit Insurance" on every order.

1

Measure Track or Rod Width

Do not measure the glass pane alone. Extend your rod 8–12 inches past the window moulding on each side so the curtains can stack cleanly without obstructing natural light.

Formula: Window Width + 16" = Rod Width
2

Determine Length & Puddle

Measure from the top of the track or rod ring eyelet straight down to the floor. Choose whether you desire a Floor Float (0.5" above floor), Touch (kissing the floor), or Opulent Puddle (+2" to +4").

Standard: Ceiling Height - 1" = Curtain Length
3

Fullness & Pleat Multiplier

Our atelier automatically incorporates a 2.0x to 2.5x fabric fullness ratio. Enter your raw rod width, and our master cutters calculate the precise pleat spacing and fabric yardage needed.

Atelier Guarantee: Millimeter Precision
